# EZTV v4.21 — Test This First

## 1. Protect the win
Watch the same normal live channel for 2–3 minutes. Confirm Smooth Live / DVR Live stability did not regress.

## 2. One live controller
While live, press OK once.
Expected: only EZTV mini guide appears. No old title/gear/record overlay underneath it.

Check D-pad focus in this order:
- Play/Pause starts pink.
- Left reaches ↶30.
- Right reaches 30↷, CC, AFR, Smooth/DVR.
- Up reaches REC / SIZE / Retry / Settings.
- Down always returns to controls/timeline/recent channels.

## 3. DVR jumps
Use DVR Live for at least 60 seconds.
- Press OK.
- Highlight ↶30 and press OK. It should NOT pause; it should jump back.
- Highlight 30↷ and press OK.
- Also test the physical FF/RW remote buttons.
A short relock after a jump is acceptable; minutes of black screen are not.

## 4. Record from mini guide
While watching DVR Live:
- OK → REC.
- Confirm Live TV keeps playing and mini guide says Recording this channel.
- OK → STOP REC.
- Confirm recording is saved to USB and plays.

## 5. Search live-channel identity
Search for the 24/7 Star Wars channel.
Play it from Search.
Then:
- channel up/down must work;
- switch elsewhere;
- use Recent Channels to return to Star Wars.

## 6. Recording/download playback focus
Play a saved recording and a saved download.
- OK → progress line.
- Up to play controls.
- Down must return to progress line.
- If you reach the title/top row, Down must return to play controls.

## 7. Download queue
Pick at least 3 episodes/movies quickly.
Downloads should show one active item and the others as Queued (#1, #2...).
Leave the screen. They should download one after another automatically.

## 8. Download Keep/Delete
Play a short downloaded item to the end.
Expected: Finished watching → Delete it / Keep it, same idea as recordings.

## 9. Poster wall
Open Movies and Series. Confirm roughly five large poster tiles across and smooth D-pad scrolling.
