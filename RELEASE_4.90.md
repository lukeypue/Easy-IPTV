# RYZOD 4.90 — Favorites

- Open Favorites from the main menu and browse all saved items, or filter by Live TV, Movies, or Series.
- Add a movie from its details, a series from its seasons screen, or a live channel from the channel's recording menu. Existing channel favorites carry over.
- Saved movies open the usual Play and Download actions, with the existing resume prompt when playback position is saved. Saved series offer Open seasons. Saved channels offer Watch and scheduled recording where supported.
- Open any saved item and choose Remove favorite. Favorites stay with their playlist across app restarts and when you switch playlists.

Fire TV uses `RYZOD-v4.90-FireTV.apk`. Android and Android TV use `RYZOD-v4.90.apk`.
