# RYZOD 4.95 — Search and Playback Controls

- Show blinking search dots while typing settles and while catalog or guide results are being found.
- Place movie and series episode Favorite actions directly after Play in the same row as Download and Close. Episode Favorite saves the entire series.
- Down opens movie and episode controls and moves to the seek timeline. Left/Right scrub the timeline, and the dedicated forward and rewind keys seek within supported media. Controls stay visible while the timeline has focus.

Fire TV uses `RYZOD-v4.95-FireTV.apk`. Android and Android TV use `RYZOD-v4.95.apk`.
