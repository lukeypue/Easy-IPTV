# RYZOD 4.97 — Picture Output Recovery

- The guide preview and full screen hand off one shared player using Media3's view switch.
- Live picture recovery retunes or reopens playback without messaging the video renderer directly.
- Seekable movies and episodes can restart the video decoder at the current position when the picture stalls while audio continues.

Fire TV uses `RYZOD-v4.97-FireTV.apk`. Android and Android TV use `RYZOD-v4.97.apk`.
